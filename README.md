# Overthewire-My-Sojourn
An overview of my challenges, successes, and what I have learned.

I think half the fun of doing these kind of challenges is to enjoy the telling of the journey.
I will be updating the repo with a new document for each challenge I complete.
This is not a walkthrough, nor a detailed list of what I am doing. 
I will be explaining the ideas I came up with, not how I used them, but rather what I learned and maybe 
some of my more glorious failures because those always make the best stories.
This is purely for bragging rights and a few possible laughs at my expense. 
I encourage anyone who stumbled across this to try out overthewire.org. 
It's a very enjoyable way to learn. I hope it inspires you to record your journey over the wire!
