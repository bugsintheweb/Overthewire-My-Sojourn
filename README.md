# Overthewire-My-Sojourn
An overview of my challenges, successes, and what I have learned.

I will be grouping in folders each game starting with bandit.
I will be updating the folder with a new document for each challenge I complete
this is not a walkthrough, nor a detailed list of what I am doing. I will be explaining tools I used, not how I used them, what I learned and maybe some of my more glorious failures because those always make the best stories.
This is purely for bragging rights and a few possible laughs at my expense. 
I encourage anyone who stumbled across this to try out overthewire.org. it's a very enjoyable way to learn. I hope it inspires you to record your journey over the wire!
